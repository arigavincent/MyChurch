Shekinah Sons Global Privacy Policy

Last updated: May 26, 2026

Shekinah Sons Global respects your privacy. This policy explains what data the app may collect, why it is used, and how it is handled.

Information we may collect

  • Account information such as your name and email address when you register or sign in.
  • User content such as prayer requests, prayer comments, testimonies, sermon notes, and profile updates that you submit.
  • Progress data such as reading progress, completed devotions, and other in-app activity tied to your account.
  • Giving records such as donation history and payment-related metadata when you use giving features.
  • Technical information required to operate the app securely, such as authentication tokens and basic request logs.

How we use information

  • To create and manage your account.
  • To sync your private progress and notes across devices.
  • To display church content such as sermons, devotions, events, and Bible features.
  • To process giving-related actions and show your giving history.
  • To maintain app security, troubleshoot issues, and improve reliability.

Data sharing

We do not sell your personal information. Data may be processed through service providers used to operate the app, such as hosting, database, media, authentication, or payment infrastructure.

Data storage and security

We take reasonable measures to protect account and church data. No method of transmission or storage is completely secure, so absolute security cannot be guaranteed.

Your choices

You may request profile changes or account-related assistance through the church administrators. If you no longer want to use the app, you may stop using it and request account support where applicable.

Children

The app is intended for general church use and is not designed as a standalone service specifically for children.

Contact

If you have questions about this policy, contact Shekinah Sons Global through the contact details provided in the app or by your church administration team.
